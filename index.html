About

Hi, I'm Zhuo Han.

I am a Research Scientist at Huawei’s Graphics and Spatial Computing Lab, part of the Institute of Media Technology and Experiment Design, Huawei 2012 Lab. I joined Huawei in March 2026.

I received my Ph.D. in Transportation Engineering from the University of Massachusetts Amherst. I also earned master's degrees in Computer Science and Transportation Engineering at UMass Amherst. I received my B.E. in Transportation from Southwest Jiaotong University in 2019.

My current work focuses on spatial intelligence, world models, and 3D reconstruction.

Research Projects

Multi-GPU Training for Dense 3DGS Reconstruction

  • Dense reconstruction: Develop multi-GPU training for dense 3DGS representations with large numbers of Gaussian primitives.
  • Parallel computation: Optimize training computation across GPUs to address the computational demands of dense reconstruction.
  • Training scalability: Aim to improve training throughput and GPU utilization while preserving reconstruction quality.

Cross-Level LOD Coding for 3DGS

  • Multiple Gaussian budgets: Design encoding and decoding methods for 3DGS representations at different levels of detail (LOD), characterized by different numbers of Gaussian primitives.
  • Cross-level redundancy: Exploit shared information and correlations across LOD levels, moving beyond independent coding of each representation.
  • Rate–distortion trade-off: Aim to reduce the compressed bitstream size while preserving reconstruction quality across levels.
